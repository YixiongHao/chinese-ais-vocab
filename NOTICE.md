# Notice on third-party content

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers this project's own code, schema, and editorial
content — the definitions, notes, pitfalls, recommendations, and the site.

It does not and cannot cover the quoted source material. `data/` contains short
passages quoted from published Chinese and English sources as terminological
evidence: what a given document actually wrote, so a reader can check the claim
rather than take it on trust. Each quotation carries its publisher, date, and a
link to the original.

Those quotations remain the property of their respective rights holders. They are
included here for reference, verification, and commentary, and are kept to the
minimum length needed to show the term in use.

If you hold rights in quoted material and want a passage shortened or removed,
open an issue and it will be handled.
